# Full-scale analysis (draft)

Draft written 2026-11-08. All numbers come from `analysis/fullscale/tables.md`, generated by `trace-pipeline/tools/fullscale_report.py` from `cluster-data/results/fullscale/`. Section names below refer to that file. Figures: `analysis/fullscale/fig_reads_per_token.png`, `fig_llc_sweep.png`, `fig_region_time.png`. This file covers the full-scale DynamoRIO route; `ANALYSIS.md` covers the gem5-era runs and is unchanged.

## 1. Method

- Models: Qwen2.5-0.5B (Q4_0), Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (Q4_K_M), Qwen3-30B-A3B (Q4_K_M, MoE with 128 experts, 8 active). llama.cpp native build with AVX-512 and 4 threads, run on node01, node03 or node06.
- Capture: DynamoRIO attaches to one decode step (a "burst") and detaches. Step 1 for short prompts (12 tokens), step 4 after an 8k prompt, step 16 after a 32k prompt. Each burst is replayed through a cold drcachesim hierarchy (22 MiB, 11-way LLC), and the LL misses plus prefetch misses become the DRAM read stream.
- Validation: per-token DRAM reads for Qwen 0.5B match the gem5 captures within 0.7% (`context.md`). Every traced run produced the same text as its untraced run (recorded in `context.md`; the report does not recheck this). The traced step's instruction count and native step time are in "Runs and validation".
- Replay: Ramulator 2.1 on the read stream, saturated, no timestamps, DDR4 (1 channel) and DDR5 (16 sub-channels), mappers RoBaRaCoCh, MOP4CLXOR, ChRaBaRoCo, policies Open and ClosedCAP. An offline in-order row-hit model covers three physical placements: VA-order packed, 4 KiB shuffled, 2 MiB (THP) shuffled.
- Attribution: separate `drattr` runs dump tensor addresses, so each DRAM read is assigned to dense weights, expert weights, KV cache, activations or unattributed.
- Region study: the attributed stream is remapped by identity, bit reversal, a global XOR and a region-aware scheme, then replayed (12M-read windows).

## 2. Findings so far

**Reads per token and the weights sweep.** At short context the model streams its weights once per token: 4.40 GB for Qwen 7B (93.9% of the 4.68 GB file) and 0.35 GB for 0.5B (81.9%). Prefetch changes the read count by under 0.3% ("DRAM reads per token"). Each compute thread's stream is sequential: +1 line is 63% of reads and 31 to 32% fall within 16 lines, with 0.4 to 1.1% far jumps ("Stride"). The merged stream looks random (60% "beyond" for 7B) only because four such streams interleave.

**Row hits.** In-order offline hit is 95.5 to 96.9% for RoRaBaCoCh and for XOR-bank at short context in all three models, and 93 to 96% for shuffled 4 KiB pages. Ramulator with RoBaRaCoCh and Open page gives 97.6 to 98.5% hits on DDR4 and DDR5. ClosedCAP drops hits to about 78% and costs 15 to 33% more time on DDR4. A column-high mapper (RoCoRaBaCh) gets 1.5 to 20%.

**DDR4 vs DDR5.** On one DDR4 channel the mapping matters for time: MOP4CLXOR is 16 to 25% faster than RoBaRaCoCh/Open despite only 38 to 63% hits, because it spreads the stream over more banks (about 17.9 GB/s against 14 to 16 GB/s). On the 16-sub-channel DDR5 it is within 2% (0.98 to 1.00), so mapping barely matters for weight sweeps. ChRaBaRoCo is worst everywhere (1.2 to 1.4x on DDR4, about 2x on DDR5).

**KV amplification at 32k.** Qwen 7B at 31001 tokens reads 9.78 GB per token, 1.58x the 6.18 GB expected (4.40 GB weights + 1.78 GB KV). The attribution run shows KV cache at 7.06 GB, about 4x its 1.78 GB size, and 61.5% of reads. At 8k the ratio is 1.01 (KV 0.42 GB, read once). The LLC sweep supports a capacity effect: at 32k the reads fall from 10.32 GB (22 MiB) to 6.99 (32), 6.21 (64) and 6.16 GB (128 MiB), close to the expected 6.18 GB; at 8k the size does not matter (4.84 to 4.80 GB). Qwen 0.5B at 32k does not amplify (0.75 GB against 0.73 expected), consistent with its per-layer KV fitting in the LLC while the 7B's does not.

**Placement.** THP (2 MiB) placement hurts KV row hits: for the 7B at 32k the KV class drops from 92.3% (packed) to 72.4% under RoRaBaCoCh, and the 0.5B KV class to 52.4%. An XOR-bank mapping recovers it to 87.0% (7B) and 96.3% (0.5B). Dense and expert weights are unaffected (96 to 97%).

**MoE.** Qwen3-30B-A3B reads 1.94 GB per token, 10.5% of its 18.56 GB file. Attribution splits this into expert weights 56.5% and dense weights 42.3%. Every expert tensor (144 of them) has exactly 8 of 128 experts touched. At 8k context the total rises to 2.70 GB (14.5%), consistent with the expected 2.67 GB.

**Literature mappings on the region replay.** Bit reversal hurts packed placements: 1.16 to 1.44x on the 7B at 8k, 1.13 to 1.28x for the 30B, up to 2.36x with ClosedCAP on the 32k window. A global XOR is neutral (0.98 to 1.01) on packed placements. It helps only where the KV is on THP pages: 0.83 (DDR5, Open) and 0.71 (DDR5, ClosedCAP) on the 32k window, 0.93 and 0.85 at 8k, 0.92 on DDR4. The region-aware scheme so far ties global XOR (for example 0.83 vs 0.83 and 0.94 vs 0.93), so there is not yet evidence for a region-specific mapping.

## 3. What the results cannot support

- **Open loop, saturated.** Ramulator ignores timestamps and issues as fast as the controller accepts. Throughputs and relative times are bandwidth-bound upper bounds, not token latencies, and the MOP-over-RoBaRaCoCh gain on DDR4 depends on that saturation.
- **No writes.** The replayed stream is reads only. KV appends, activations written back and writeback-induced bus turnarounds are missing.
- **One token per configuration.** Each row is a single decode step. Variation across tokens (position, expert routing) is not measured, and the MoE expert choice is one sample.
- **Discrepancy between the two 32k 7B runs.** The plain burst reads 9.78 GB (and its LL-sweep twin 10.32 GB) while the `drattr` run attributes 179.5M lines, 11.5 GB. The cause is not established. The attribution shares and the 4x KV amplification come from the 11.5 GB run, so the amplification factor is uncertain by about 15 to 20% until this is explained. (The attr run may use different instrumentation or a different step; see `context.md`.)
- **Cold caches per window**, a single LLC configuration for the headline numbers, and the region comparison uses 12M-read windows (for 32k the "early" window), not the whole token.
- **Unexplained:** the 7B at 32k on 16-channel DDR5 reaches 142 GB/s against about 270 GB/s for short context, which suggests channel or bank imbalance in the KV stream. Not yet investigated.
